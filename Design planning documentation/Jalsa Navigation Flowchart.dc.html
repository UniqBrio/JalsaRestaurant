Jalsa Restaurant · Hosur

Navigation Flowchart

Every screen in the system, who reaches it, and what leads where. Four surfaces run at once — the guest's phone, the captain's phone, the owner's desktop and the kitchen printer — and most of the work happens where they hand off to each other. Those handoffs are set out in section five.

Guest phone

Entered by scanning a QR. No sign-in, no app. 11 screens plus 7 sheets.

Captain and waiter

Phone, PIN sign-in. 5 sections, scoped to assigned tables.

Owner and admin

Desktop, PIN sign-in. 13 sections; Settings holds 10 more.

Kitchen

No screen. Paper tickets from three printers, plus a print-history trail.

Section one

Guest — the ordering journey

One straight line with three places to leave it and come back. The QR is a fixed address, so closing the browser loses nothing: rescanning resolves the table's open bill and returns the guest to wherever they were.

Scan table QR→ Welcome→ Menu→ Cart→ Order placed→ My order→ Request payment→ Offer strip→ Tip→ Paying→ Paid→ Bill
LOOP
My order→Order more→Menu→new KOT on the same bill
FAILURE
Paying→Payment failed→Try again·Pay the captain instead
SERVED
Captain marks served→hearts unlock→Loved it sheet→parcel as a separate takeaway bill
RESCAN
Scan again, bill open→My order, restored|bill closed, inside the window→Paid|window passed→fresh Welcome

Position comes from the moment Join is tapped, stamped by the server, so simultaneous scans still get separate places. The pair name is what gets called at the door — the only layer that reaches a guest with no phone or a closed page.

Section two

Captain and waiter — the floor

Five sections on a phone, all scoped to assigned tables. Signing in with a PIN is what puts a name against every order, discount and closure that follows.

PIN sign-in→ Tables→ Table detail→ Add items· Change quantity· Cancel with reason· Mark served· Request closure
TablesAssigned tables with status — free, ordering, in the kitchen, ready, served, payment requested, clearing. Opens table detail.
ReadyRounds the kitchen has marked ready, oldest first. Tapping one marks it served and unlocks the guest's hearts.
KOTsEvery ticket for the assigned tables, with print state. Reprint marks the ticket as a reprint.
RequestsWater, bottles, plates, parcels and call-captain, grouped by type with a count. Marking one done clears it from the owner's dashboard too.
MeOwn tables, tips attributed so far, module access granted, sign out.
The five identifiers — captain, table, bill number, waiter, KOT — appear in the same order and the same place on every screen that touches an order, and on the printed ticket. Staff cross-reference constantly; a stable spine means never hunting for the same number twice.
Section three

Owner — thirteen sections

Desktop, PIN sign-in. The dashboard is the landing screen and every figure on it links to the section that itemises it.

DashboardSales, orders, payment mix, tips, waiting count, open requests, alerts. Every card navigates and pre-applies its filter.
Day setupWho is in today, which machines are up, a notice to pass on. Same-as-yesterday shortcut. Today only — permanent values live in Settings.
Live ordersEvery open bill and its rounds. Opens bill detail: identifier strip, KOT list with provenance and timings, running total.
WaitlistThe queue in order, reachability per party, Notify, Call, No-show, Seat. Seating reads each table's seat count.
PaymentsBills awaiting closure and closed today. Record payment carries the discount controls and, for a group, a per-table breakdown.
MenuSearch, categories, items. Item editor holds price, food type, image, serving windows, available days and closure duration.
StaffPeople, roles, module access, PIN issuance, paperwork, removal with reason. Feeds the HR documents.
TipsTotal and staff-wise, settlement recording. Excluded from revenue and stated as such.
ExpensesEntry, edit and delete, each with a reason and an audit entry. Feeds the purchases and final reports.
ReportsSales, purchases and expenses, final report, all-orders ledger. One date range governs every panel; each exports.
UpliftRevenue the system itself created — repeat rounds, upsells, tips, parcels — over the chosen range, with its own export.
SettingsTen sub-sections, listed below.
Audit logDate, time, action, what changed, bill or table, and who. Type filters, cross-column search, sortable columns.

Section six

Printing and paperwork

Print setup — five sections

Overview· Printers· Templates· Routing· History

Three machines: main kitchen and counter at 80 mm, tandoor station at 58 mm. Templates are character grids, so the two widths are separate layouts rather than one scaled picture, and validation blocks a save that would print clipped. Routing runs category to station to printer, with the food-type split a separate decision.

HR documents

Staff record→ Offer letter· Experience certificate· Payslip

Variables merge from the employment record and the restaurant identity. Anything unfilled prints as a marked placeholder so nothing is signed blank.

Two rules that shape the whole chart. A bill belongs to one or more tables, so an ordinary table is a group of one and nothing special-cases it. And the guest never marks a bill paid — they submit a payment request, and a named member of staff records the closure. Every screen above follows from those two.
