## The shape, as a reminder only

1. **Path test** — app feature code only? → APP-ONLY, stop, seconds.
2. **Domain-word test** — can the rule be stated with zero business words? Grep it against the
   app's `PRODUCT_LEXICON.md`.
3. **Rule of three** — n=1 parks in `docs/registers/CANDIDATES.md`; n=2 from a *different* app
   promotes via `/framework-update`.

**Default verdict: APP-ONLY.** Argue the case; a human decides. On PROMOTE, the framework change
carries the full quadruple close-out (process + flow + cases + version) and must pass
conformance against the fixtures.
